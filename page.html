WELCOME TO LIMERICK GAA

History of Limerick Hurling Team

The Limerick County Hurling Team was founded in 1884, it was founded by the Gaelic Athletic Association, known as the GAA. Limerick's first outright success in hurling was achieved when the Kilfinane club defeated Kilkenny GAA club Tullaroan in the final of the 1897 All-Ireland Senior Hurling Championship. Limerick won the 1918 All-Ireland SHC and the won again in the 1921 All-Ireland SHC, winning the Liam MacCarthy Cup.
