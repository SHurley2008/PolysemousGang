WELCOME TO LIMERICK GAA

History

The Limerick County Hurling Team was founded in 1884 by the Gaelic Athletic Association, known as the GAA. Limerick's first outright success in hurling was achieved when the Kilfinane club defeated Kilkenny GAA club Tullaroan in the final of the 1897 All-Ireland Senior Hurling Championship. Limerick won the 1918 All-Ireland SHC and again in 1921, winning the Liam MacCarthy Cup.

The 1930s were known as the salad days of Limerick hurling. The team won five consecutive Munster SHC titles, a feat achieved only by Limerick and Cork. After winning All-Ireland SHC titles in 1934 and 1936, Limerick won another in 1940. The team helped raise the profile of the sport: around 30,000 people attended the 1930 All-Ireland SHC Final.

Limerick won the 1970–71 NHL title and soon after won the 1973 All-Ireland SHC, their 17th title. Four further NHL titles followed: 1983–84, 1984–85, 1991–92 and 1997.

In the early 2000s, Limerick won three consecutive All-Ireland Under-21 Hurling Championship titles. In 2007, Limerick defeated Tipperary in a Munster SHC semi-final known as the Trilogy, as three games were required to separate the teams. The second replay ended 0–22 to 2–13. An attendance of 30,608 witnessed Limerick defeat Tipperary for the first time since 1996. Limerick had not won a Munster SHC match since a 2001 comeback against Waterford. The team then reached a Munster SHC final, which they lost to Tipperary, the “gorilla” on their back, as Seamus Hickey referred to them.

Limerick were drawn against Clare in the first round of the 2008 Munster SHC, but Clare won 4–12 to 1–16, eliminating Limerick. The result sent Limerick into a game against Offaly under the revised All-Ireland SHC qualifying system. Limerick lost 3–19 to 0–18, ending their season.

Limerick opened the 2009 Munster SHC against Waterford in a draw, then narrowly lost the replay. In the All-Ireland SHC qualifiers, they defeated Wexford and Laois, then Dublin in a quarter-final, to reach a semi-final against Tipperary. Tipperary won by 22 points. In October 2011, John Allen was appointed manager. After defeats to Clare in the Division 1B Final and Tipperary in the Munster SHC, a summer fitness course helped the team improve. A creditable loss to Kilkenny in the 2012 All-Ireland SHC quarter-final offered hope for 2013. As in 2012, Dublin, managed by Anthony Daly, defeated Limerick in the 2013 NHL Division 1B Final.

The 2018 season concluded with Limerick winning the All-Ireland SHC, their first since 1973. They defeated Galway 3–16 to 2–18 in the final. The team built on this success, winning the NHL in 2019, 2020 and 2023; the Munster SHC in 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022; and the All-Ireland SHC again in 2020, 2021 and 2022.
